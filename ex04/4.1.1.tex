/subsection{Debunking the 100X GPU vs. CPU Myth: An Evaluation of Throughput Computing on CPU and GPU}
This article successfully shows that GPUs are not unlike advertised 100-1000x faster than CPUs but more in the ballpark of 2.5\% faster. This was done by systematically comparing different throughput-kernels and optimizing them for GPU as well as CPU. The paper therefore showed how important fair treatment optimizations is to get scientific results.\\
The misconception around the alleged advantage of GPU-performance originated in the unfair comparison of underpowered and not optimized CPU code with highly GPU-programs. But actually both CPU and GPU can profit form platform specific optimizations, which closes the gap between their performance. Also the biggest performance difference does not originate on the raw compute power of these devices but more storage-speed, cache-size, data access patterns as well as the cost of synchronization.\\
In our opinion this article is very important because it aligns the expectations of GPU-computing more with the reality of the actual expected performance. The methodology seems fair, even though the researchers are from Intel they did optimizations of the GPU as well. Altogether this article shows nicely that it is more important to optimize for the architecture you work with than the architecture itself.
