\subsection{cudaMemcpy}

In this Task, the use of cudaMalloc(\&hmem, dat); and cudaMemcpy(hmem, dmem, dat, cudaMemcpyDeviceToDevice); was analyzed. That way we copy data from device to device. This happends of around 263 GB/s. This is also, because on the device overhead dominates at small datasizes. We are only able to get close to peak bandwith once we reach larger data sizes, approching 1GB/s.  The D2D vastly outperforms the approximately 10GB/s we get on D2H or H2D over PCIe.
